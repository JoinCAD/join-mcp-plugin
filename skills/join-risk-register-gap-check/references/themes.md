# Risk themes

Assign every risk exactly one **primary** theme, by its root cause or subject, not by incidental words. A risk named "Structural steel lead times" is about procurement, not structure. "Wet weather delaying excavation" is about weather, not schedule. "Builder's risk policy for Phase 2" is about insurance, not phasing.

Use these names exactly; the comparison script rejects anything else.

| Theme | What belongs here |
|---|---|
| Design Completeness & Changes | Incomplete or late design, design creep, pending design decisions, discipline coordination and clashes, delegated design, code-driven design questions (ADA, rated corridors, egress), RFIs and submittal problems |
| MEP & Building Systems | Mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, low-voltage, AV/IT, security, medical gas, generators and switchgear as systems (their design, scope or capacity), elevators, specialty equipment like headwalls |
| Permitting & Agency Approvals | Permits, entitlements, zoning, plan check, AHJ / fire marshal / state health or school agencies, DOT and right-of-way approvals, regulatory interpretations, inspections by authorities |
| Utilities & Site Services | Utility service connections, capacity of power/gas/water/sewer/storm/telecom, utility routing and relocation, utility-company schedules, easements |
| Existing Conditions & Renovation | Existing buildings and systems, inaccurate as-builts, demolition, tie-ins, historic fabric, hazardous materials in existing construction, work in occupied facilities, shutdowns, infection control |
| Geotechnical & Unforeseen Site Conditions | Soils, rock, groundwater, dewatering, contaminated ground, undocumented fill, unknown buried utilities or obstructions, archaeology |
| Procurement & Long-Lead | Lead times, equipment availability, supply chain, buyout, bidder coverage, sole-source items, delivery delays |
| Owner Scope & Program Changes | Owner-driven program, scope, allowance or user-group changes; owner-furnished equipment decisions |
| Logistics & Site Access | Site access, laydown, hoisting and cranes, traffic, neighbors, adjacent construction, disruption to occupants or the public, noise and dust |
| Budget & Funding | Budget adequacy, funding and approvals, GMP, contingency, estimate gaps, savings targets, fees |
| Contract & Insurance | Contract terms, insurance, bonds, liquidated damages, indemnity, claims, warranties, insurer requirements, payment terms, retainage |
| Schedule & Phasing | Overall duration, phasing, sequencing, milestones, acceleration, fixed opening dates (when the schedule itself is the subject) |
| Safety & Quality | Safety, QA/QC, testing, inspections, surveys, commissioning, training |
| Envelope & Finishes | Exterior skin, roofing, glazing, curtain wall, waterproofing, interior finishes, casework, doors, signage, mock-ups |
| Weather & Environment | Weather, seasons, flood, wildfire smoke, heat, stormwater, wetlands and protected species |
| Seismic & Structural | Structural design and loads, foundations, piles, shoring, steel erection, seismic design and bracing |
| Material Cost Escalation | Price inflation or volatility of materials and commodities (other than tariffs) |
| Labor & Workforce | Labor availability, staffing, trade capacity, union or prevailing wage, workforce participation goals |
| Tariffs & Trade Policy | Tariffs, import duties, trade policy, domestic-content requirements |
| Energy Code & Sustainability | Energy code, certification targets (LEED etc.), net zero, electrification, PV and battery as sustainability measures |

## Two categories that are excluded from the comparison

Put these rows under one of these labels. They are counted and reported, but they don't count toward the register.

- **Scope Alternates & Value Engineering**: entries that are really VE options, bid alternates or add/deduct items rather than risks. Examples: "X in lieu of Y", "Alt #3 – sound masking", "Reduce brick at north elevation".
- **Uncategorized**: placeholders or entries too vague to classify ("Risk #2", "test", "TBD"). This also covers pre-bid go/no-go checklist questions copied onto the register (e.g. "Is the retainage clause unusual?"). Those are a bid-review checklist, not project risks. If a register contains many of them, say so in the report.

## Tie-breakers

- Equipment such as generators, switchgear or AHUs goes to **Procurement & Long-Lead** when the risk is availability or lead time. It goes to **MEP & Building Systems** when the risk is design, sizing or scope.
- Fire-flow or water-pressure shortfalls from the municipal supply are **Utilities & Site Services**. An added fire pump or sprinkler scope is **MEP & Building Systems**.
- Owner-requested changes are **Owner Scope & Program Changes**. Designer-originated gaps or late drawings are **Design Completeness & Changes**.
- Contaminated soil, including its disposal, is **Geotechnical & Unforeseen Site Conditions**. Hazardous materials inside an existing building are **Existing Conditions & Renovation**.
- Non-English entries: translate mentally and classify normally.
